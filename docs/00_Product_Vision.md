# 4CoreFinSupport: Product Vision
- **Platform Name:** 4CoreFinSupport
- **Tagline:** FinTech Incident Control & Payment Operations Intelligence
- **Core Mission:** Redefining payment operations from a simple reactive ticketing model into a unified, proactive intelligence platform.
- **Standards & Compliances:** 
  - **ISO 10002:** Standard-compliant complaint-handling system featuring mandatory business validation before closure.
  - **NDPA 2023:** Strong PII data protections and consent rules.
  - **GAID 2025:** High security, structural data masking, and 10-year immutable audit ledgers.
