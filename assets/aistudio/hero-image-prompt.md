# AI Image Generation Prompt: Enterprise Landing Page Hero

## Objective
Generate a high-end, premium, and modern enterprise-grade hero illustration for an "Enterprise Operations & Compliance Hub". The image must communicate professionalism, trust, and digital transformation.

## Design Language
- **Style**: High-end 3D Isometric Illustration (similar to Stripe, Linear, or Microsoft 365).
- **Vibe**: Clean, sophisticated, future-ready, and expensive.
- **Composition**: Centered, uncluttered, landscape orientation, optimized for a hero banner.
- **Avoid**: Generic stock photos, cartoons, clipart, busy backgrounds, or text inside the image.

## Visual Elements to Incorporate
- **Central Theme**: A connected ecosystem of business operations.
- **Elements**: 
    - Floating holographic digital dashboards with sleek financial charts and analytics.
    - Abstract representation of workflow automation (connected nodes, data flow lines).
    - Subtle icons representing HR, Legal, Finance, and Procurement.
    - A professional, minimal office environment as a soft background.
    - A security shield or lock symbol signifying "Compliance" and "Governance".
    - Document stacks transitioning into digital data streams.

## Color Palette (Brand Aligned)
- **Primary**: Dark Red (#8B0000) - Use for key highlights, accents, and focal points.
- **Secondary**: Pure White (#FFFFFF) - Use for clean surfaces and negative space.
- **Accent**: Soft Gray (#F0F1F3) - Use for secondary elements and subtle backgrounds.
- **Lighting**: Soft, diffuse studio lighting with subtle gradients and minimal, elegant shadows.

## Technical Specifications
- **Resolution**: 4K Ultra High Definition.
- **Quality**: Vector-like precision, sharp edges, smooth surfaces.
- **Background**: Transparent background preferred (or extremely light gray #F8F9FA).
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 / Landscape.
